Space Facts

🌌 Milky Way
Our galaxy contains over 100 billion stars and spans about 100,000 light-years in diameter!
🌞 Sun
The Sun accounts for 99.8% of the Solar System’s mass and is about 4.6 billion years old.
🪐 Saturn
Saturn’s rings are mostly made of ice and rock, stretching thousands of kilometers but only a few meters thick!
🌍 Earth
70% of Earth’s surface is covered in water, and it’s the only planet known to support life.
🪐 Jupiter
Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System and has a massive storm called the Great Red Spot that’s been raging for centuries.
🌠 Shooting Stars
These are meteors burning up in Earth’s atmosphere, creating bright streaks in the night sky.
🛰 Hubble Telescope
It has captured some of the most detailed images of distant galaxies and cosmic phenomena since 1990.
🌑 Moon
Earth’s only natural satellite influences tides and has a surface covered in craters from billions of years of impacts.
🌠 Meteor Shower
Meteor showers occur when Earth passes through debris left by comets, creating spectacular streaks of light in the sky.
🌌 Andromeda Galaxy
The Andromeda Galaxy is the closest spiral galaxy to the Milky Way and will collide with it in about 4.5 billion years!
