About Steller Smarts

Welcome to Steller Smarts, a thrilling quiz that challenges your knowledge of astronauts, space exploration, and the vast mysteries of the universe! Whether you're an aspiring astronaut or simply fascinated by space, our quiz is designed to entertain and educate.

Our Mission

We aim to inspire curiosity about space, increase awareness of astronauts' incredible achievements, and provide an engaging platform to learn while having fun. At Steller Smarts, we believe that understanding the cosmos is both exciting and essential for the future of humanity.

Our Team

We are a passionate group of space enthusiasts, quiz lovers, and educators. Our goal is to create quizzes that are not only fun but also educational, allowing everyone to explore the world of space and astronauts through challenging and entertaining questions.
